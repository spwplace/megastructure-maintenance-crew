# The Crew
## Characters Document

---

## Overview

You are part of a maintenance squad—one of many throughout the structure, though you rarely see the others. Your squad is your world. These are the people you work with, eat with, sleep near, survive alongside. You know them too well. You can't escape them. You wouldn't want to.

The player character is **you**—unnamed, unvoiced, defined by choices and presence rather than backstory. The crew around you carries the personality, the history, the warmth.

---

## The Squad

### KETH
**Role:** Senior Technician (de facto squad lead)
**Age:** Late 40s
**Specialty:** Mechanical systems, structural repair

**Overview:**
Keth has been doing this work for nearly thirty years. They've seen three generations of crew members come through. They don't give orders—that's not how the squad works—but when Keth suggests something, people listen.

**Personality:**
- Quiet, economical with words
- Dark humor delivered completely deadpan
- Protective of the squad without being sentimental about it
- Has made peace with the endlessness of the work—or seems to have

**Key Traits:**
- Knows the structure better than anyone. Has routes that shouldn't work but do.
- Never talks about the enemy directly. Changes the subject.
- Keeps a small collection of objects found during repairs—a habit they never explain.
- Sleeps less than anyone but never seems tired.

**Rumors They Share:**
"I've heard a lot of theories about what's out there. Doesn't matter. Whatever it is, it bleeds the same way we do—damage, repairs, damage. They've got a crew like us. Probably hate their jobs too."

---

### SOLENNE
**Role:** Biological Systems Technician
**Age:** Early 30s
**Specialty:** Grow-decks, algae vats, atmospheric bio-filters

**Overview:**
Solenne works with living systems—the parts of the structure that grow, breathe, die, and need to be convinced rather than commanded. They talk to plants. They claim the plants talk back.

**Personality:**
- Warm, prone to rambling
- Finds wonder in biological processes others find disgusting
- Anxious about mechanical work—prefers things that heal
- Holds onto hope more visibly than the others

**Key Traits:**
- Names the plants. Mourns them when they die.
- Has theories about the fungal networks—thinks they're becoming something
- The most likely to ask if you're okay, mean it, and actually wait for an answer
- Terrible at navigation. Gets lost constantly. Laughs about it.

**Rumors They Share:**
"Someone in Block F said the enemy doesn't have bio-systems. All machine. That's why they need us—they want our grow-decks, our air, our *life*. We're not at war, we're being *harvested*."

---

### DAURO
**Role:** Fluid Systems Technician
**Age:** Mid 20s
**Specialty:** Pipes, pumps, circulatory systems, waste processing

**Overview:**
Dauro is the newest member of the squad—only two years in. Still young enough to be frustrated by things the others have accepted. Still asking "why" when the answer is "because."

**Personality:**
- Talkative, curious, occasionally annoying
- Hasn't learned to pace themselves—burns hot, crashes hard
- Questions the logic of things (the structure's, the siege's, the work's)
- Idealistic in a way that hasn't been fully ground down yet

**Key Traits:**
- Takes notes. Keeps a journal. Trying to make sense of the structure's layout.
- Gets into arguments with Keth about "the right way" to do things
- Has a theory that the structure was originally two structures, fused
- The most likely to suggest there might be a way to end the siege

**Rumors They Share:**
"Okay, but listen—my cousin's squad, three sectors over? They said the last attack didn't feel like an attack. It felt like *docking*. Like something was trying to connect. What if they're not trying to destroy us?"

---

### VELL
**Role:** Electrical Systems Technician
**Age:** Late 30s
**Specialty:** Wiring, sensors, data systems, power routing

**Overview:**
Vell works with the structure's nervous system—the parts that sense, signal, and remember. Most of these systems are degraded beyond repair. Vell coaxes function from decay.

**Personality:**
- Sardonic, deflects with jokes
- Privately melancholic—hides it well, not perfectly
- Deeply skilled, doesn't like to be praised for it
- The one who remembers birthdays, shift anniversaries, small kindnesses

**Key Traits:**
- Has accessed parts of the structure's old data systems. Won't say what they found.
- Makes small electronic trinkets in their off-hours. Gifts them to crew.
- Insomniac. Often found in the corridors during sleep shift, "checking readings."
- Had a partner who transferred to another squad. Doesn't talk about it.

**Rumors They Share:**
"There's data in the old systems. Fragments. I found a file once—just numbers, coordinates maybe. They were labeled 'home.' But here's the thing: the file was modified. Recently. Something is still updating."

---

### ORRIN
**Role:** General Maintenance / Damage Response
**Age:** 50s
**Specialty:** Emergency repair, hull breaches, hazard zones

**Overview:**
Orrin does the jobs no one else wants—the fast, ugly, dangerous work when a sector is failing and there's no time for caution. They're the first into a breach and the last out.

**Personality:**
- Blunt, no patience for hesitation
- Fatalistic but not nihilistic—they believe the work matters, just not that they'll survive it
- Respects competence, despises excuses
- Surprisingly gentle in rare quiet moments

**Key Traits:**
- Covered in scars. Doesn't explain them.
- Has been in The Wound more than anyone. Says it's "not that bad."
- The only one who speaks of the enemy with something like respect
- Volunteers for the worst shifts. Refuses to explain why.

**Rumors They Share:**
"I was in a breach once, early in the siege. Pressure was venting, couldn't see anything. And for a second—just a second—I saw something through the gap. A light. Moving. Not random. *Looking*. Then the seal went up and it was gone. I don't know what I saw. I don't want to know."

## Crew Dynamics

### Relationships

```
        KETH
       /    \
      /      \
   ORRIN    SOLENNE
     |        |
     |        |
   VELL ---- DAURO
```

- **Keth & Orrin:** Long history. Trust built through decades. Communicate in glances.
- **Keth & Solenne:** Keth is protective. Solenne is one of the few who can make Keth laugh.
- **Orrin & Vell:** Mutual respect, but tension. Vell thinks Orrin takes unnecessary risks.
- **Vell & Dauro:** Vell has taken Dauro under their wing. Sees something familiar in them.
- **Solenne & Dauro:** Both idealists, but Solenne's hope is quieter. They balance each other.

### The Player's Position

The player is **new enough** to need guidance but **present enough** to have opinions. The crew knows you. They've worked with you. But there's still space for them to tell you things, explain things, reveal things.

Your choices shape which crew members you're closer to—not through a visible "affection meter" but through which conversations you pursue, which shifts you volunteer for, who you sit with during downtime.

---

## Dialogue Philosophy

### How the Crew Talks

- **Overlapping references** — they mention people, places, events without full explanation. You piece together context.
- **Work as language** — they express care through helping, criticism through technique, affection through tools lent.
- **Silences mean things** — not everything needs to be said. Shared labor communicates.
- **Rumors are texture** — crew members share theories about the enemy, the structure, each other. None are confirmed. All reveal character.

### Tone Examples

**KETH:** "Seal's holding. Good enough. Let's move before good enough becomes not enough."

**SOLENNE:** "The moss is stressed—see how it's curling? It knows something's wrong. It always knows before we do."

**DAURO:** "But that doesn't make *sense*. The pipe goes there, the tank is here, there's no—how does it—"

**VELL:** "Don't think about it. Seriously. I tried to map it once. Lost three days. Just walk the route."

**ORRIN:** "You want to know what's out there? No you don't. Fix the seal. That's what's real. The rest is noise."

## Character Arcs (Light Touch)

This isn't a story about dramatic transformation. These are people enduring. But over the course of the game, small shifts can occur:

- **Keth** might share something about why they stayed on the squad so long
- **Solenne** might face a biological system they can't save
- **Dauro** might start to understand why the others stopped asking "why"
- **Vell** might reveal what they found in the old data systems
- **Orrin** might explain what they saw in the breach—or refuse to, more meaningfully

These arcs are **optional**, **player-driven**, and **ambiguous in resolution**. There are no "good endings" for characters. There's just more understanding, or less.
